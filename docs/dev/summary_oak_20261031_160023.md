# Mare Liberum - summary, worktree oak, 2026-10-31 (0.22.3 -> 0.22.6)

Four rounds in one day, driven by three issue folders in `assets/issues`.

## 0.22.3 - the 0.22.2 regressions undone
World height restored with a peak boost, river carving and canyons switched off, the sea made
blue-grey with transparent shallows, the authored lantina restored, Peljesac and the Dalmatian
islands authored by hand, the hinterland painted as land-use masks with low-poly props.

## 0.22.4 - the world package and the coast from data
`assets/data_chunks_terrain/world-game-package` became both the terrain and the **coastline**:
the land mask follows the overview cell by cell, ports protected, straits re-cut. Rounder
shores, the drawn waterline following the 3 km sample inside the 7 km cell. Issue folder 1:
the "ugly circle", camera shake, mast thickness, sterncastle sails, wind in the canvas, the
point-of-sail curve. The Meshy sea tiles replaced the old sea textures.

## 0.22.5 - issue folder 2
Light-plane shadow snapping (the close-terrain flicker), the seabed drawn under every visible
shallow, the brown sliver traced to a gap between the sea's two tessellation lattices, forest
and village props, the ocean checklist answered item by item, LOD guard for wrong-node ship
parts, oars pitched into the water.

## 0.22.6 - issue folder 3
The fleet moved into `assets/ships/ship_classes.json`; the lantina laced to its sail with
robands, a foot rope, a sheet and a tack; two suits of sails and two conditions of cordage per
ship from the Meshy sheets; a Kelvin wake as geometry with its own foam; screen-space
reflections of the hulls; breakers that march shoreward; the terrain pyramid artefact removed
and the world 20% lower; forests in stands; bigger, varied fields; fences, standing corn,
windmills, pitheads and boulders; card LODs for the props; sternway when head to wind.

## What to do next
`docs/ROADMAP_0.23.md`. The short version: a finer DEM for the Adriatic is the single biggest
lever and needs no code; then terrain self-shadow; then the physics foundation; then naval VFX;
then the procedural city and the shipbuilding economy.

## Known limits worth repeating
- The terrain's remaining lumpiness is the 2.8 km sample spacing, not the shading.
- Rivers are painted only, waiting on the authored data.
- The props cast no shadows and stop at 40 units; past that the painted masks carry them.
- Rigging "premium/used" is two cordage colours, not two textures: at a 1-2 px rope width a
  texture reads as noise.
- `game/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` has the full list.

## How to verify
From the session mirror: `tools-bat\build.bat [test]`, `shot.bat lon lat dist yaw file secs
[tilt]`, `rig.bat dist yaw file`, `cine.bat dist yaw ox oz pitch file`, `mask.bat lon0,lat1,w,h`.
Convert captures with `python3 -I tools/ppm2png.py in.ppm out.png --half`.
