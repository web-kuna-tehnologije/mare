# Mare Liberum - state of the work, worktree oak, 2026-10-31 (0.22.3 -> 0.22.5)

## Where the game stands
- **World**: the terrain-v2 `world-game-package` (2.8 km overview, 4 LODs) is the terrain and,
  since 0.22.4, the coastline: the land mask follows the data cell by cell (ports protected),
  the rings round the shore and follow the 3 km sample inside the 7 km cell. The height
  calibration is 20 units per 9000 m with a +35% boost above 700 m. Rivers are painted, not
  carved, until the authored river data arrives. A 0.7 km Dalmatia set exists in the
  exporter's folder and is not yet used.
- **Sea**: blue-grey body, seabed through the water by measured thickness, Meshy ripple /
  displacement / foam tiles, tessellated patches (cap 16), Gerstner sets by wind, whitecaps
  masked by the foam tile, surf with a breaker clock, bow waves, zoom-gated detail. Missing:
  reflections of the hulls (SSR), a trailing wake (off by design).
- **Ships**: the authored Meshy fleet with the lantina asset, split square yards, slimmed
  masts (0.425), aft stations lifted over the sterncastle, canvas with wind, oars that dip,
  a point-of-sail speed curve. Wrong-node LODs are now skipped by the loader.
- **Hinterland**: land-use ring texture (fields, vineyards, orchards/olives, villages) painted
  as district-aligned patterns; forest canopy paint; instanced forest trees, pines, orchard
  trees, vines, sheep and village houses near the camera, no shadows.
- **Process**: each session builds in its own `C:\projects\mare_<worktree>_<stamp>` with a
  private copy of the assets; the window title says which worktree is running.
