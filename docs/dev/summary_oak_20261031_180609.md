## What changed (0.22.8)

**The broadside, as the reference frame has it.** Three smoke layers per gun - a white cumulus
core that rolls out with the shot, a grey plume behind it, a thin haze that lingers downwind -
plus the flash and embers; a ring of flattened water and a spout of spray under a low gun; and
a shockwave per three guns that the tonemap bends and radially blurs the frame with for a
third of a second (`hdr_set_shocks`, `hdr/tonemap.frag`).

**Cordage with the Meshy rope tiles.** The geometry stage that widens the ropes writes a rope
UV; `meshes.frag` lays the new-hemp or tarred tile along it with a procedural twist, fading
out where a rope is a line again.

**Masts in proportion.** Every mast now has the main's girth (the radial slim divided by the
height ratio) and the fore and mizzen masts are taller in `ship_classes.json`. The lantina was
checked in Blender: the mast library's lateen masts do not contain their yards, the lantina is
its own asset - nothing to split.

**The giant spar (issue5.png).** Not reproduced; the likely cause (a yard placed while its asset
still streams, with zero bounds and therefore a huge scale) is closed - yards, lantinas and
the bowsprit need a loaded asset with real bounds, and a spar longer than the hull is
suppressed and logged with its asset and extents.

**800k hulls.** The eight hull classes re-imported through Blender with LOD 0 at 800,000
triangles and 2048 px base colour (`tools/meshy_import.py --lod0 800000 --tex 2048`), for the
battle and city tiers only. Texture quality in this renderer is vertex colour density.

**The strategic map.** Lowland coasts and coastal heights from the data (the cliff class and
the relief behind the shore read the data's height, not the 7 km cell's); a closer zoom with
the look-at point riding the ground; dense pine forests.
