## What changed (0.22.7)

**The battle's own clock.** Space, 1-4 and -/+ in the battle view act on a separate battle
time (stop to x8); the strategic clock is untouched. Waves, cloth, flags, oars and smoke all
follow it through a new scene clock (`RF.anim_time`), so a stopped battle is a still frame.

**Wind in the battle.** Read once at the battle's position; shown in a battle HUD as knots
and compass point; arrows over the water (N toggles); each side's line sails at the pace the
point-of-sail curve gives its heading; the smoke drifts in it.

**The coast from the world.** A probe finds the nearest land around the battle, its bearing,
the land cover and relief from the terrain package, and builds a heightfield coast at that
bearing: beach profile under the water, beach, dunes or a bank, hills or ridges, fields,
rock, snow, trees. The sea shaders share the profile, so the breakers, surf and swash run up
this sand. No land within ~56 km: no coast and a thick haze (fog of war). Desert shores are
sand and dunes with palms in the south.

**Camera.** The look-at point has a height (R/F): the eye climbs over the fleet or sits at
deck level. Eye held above the water.

**Particles.** `gfx/particles.cc`: instanced billboards with wind, buoyancy, gravity, sorted,
soft against hulls; 3D-noise "volumetric" smoke with a sun side and a back-lit rim; a flash
and embers per gun. Count and size follow the class's guns and length.
